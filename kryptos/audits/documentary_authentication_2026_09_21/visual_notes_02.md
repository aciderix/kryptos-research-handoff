## IMG_1543

The scan is a typed document headed `JIM SANBORN`, dated `July 15, 1994`, and titled `INVOICE #1`. It specifies `Waterjet cutting of stainless steel mirror`. The typed instructions visibly include:

- `TEXT TO BE MIRROR IMAGE WORDS`
- `1. Lines of text use mirror image letters.`
- `2. Letters to be 1/2" high with small spacing between words.`
- `3. words run off end of line.`
- `4. Line spacing to be 1 1/2 times 1/4" word spacing.`
- `5. 1" boarder around text.`

Handwritten text includes `USE THE LETTERS FUMEE ENGLISH ALPHABET`, with a mirrored-looking example. The page is dated four years after Kryptos’ 1990 dedication, and the visible document does not identify Kryptos or K4. It may document Sanborn’s later use of mirror-reversed text, but it cannot be transferred to K4 without project-specific provenance.

Classification: **AUTHENTIC DATED PRODUCTION DOCUMENT; KRYPTOS/K4 RELEVANCE UNESTABLISHED; NOT A K4 RECIPE**.
