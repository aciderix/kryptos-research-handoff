# Current K4 Experimental Frontier

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Status:** No new admissible experiment identified

## Current conclusion

No new K4 experiment in the current registry simultaneously passes both mandatory gates:

1. **Determination:** an independent artefact or documented fact fixes the representation, alignment, traversal, parameters, operation, and final transformation before the result is observed.
2. **Novelty:** the canonical transformation is not already covered by an equivalent internal or external family.

This is a current research state, not a claim that K4 has no solution and not a claim that the remaining open cells are false.

## What remains excluded from execution

The following classes remain recorded as hypotheses or prior art, but are not admissible as new experiments in their present form:

- 7×14 physical-tableau reconstructions that begin from a proposed plaintext;
- extra-L or two-sided overlays whose downstream comparison and alphabet operation are unspecified;
- Morse-E masks whose physical projection or extraction convention is not fixed;
- width-21 and `DESPARATLY` extraction ideas without a uniquely specified second cryptographic system;
- misspelling-derived primers without an independently fixed key-extension rule;
- anomaly-derived routes where the start, direction, or route order remains selectable.

## Required trigger for reopening the search

A new candidate may enter the experimental registry only when a genuinely new independent fact supplies the missing determination. Examples include a documented Sanborn statement that specifies an operation, a verified physical layout that fixes a coordinate map, a K1–K3 procedure whose transfer to K4 is exact rather than analogical, or a historically documented construction constraint that uniquely determines the mechanism.

The absence of an admissible experiment is preserved as information. No calculation will be performed merely to increase the experiment count, and no open cell will be promoted by inventing parameters or selecting the most coherent output afterward.

## Evidence-state chain

```text
fact → interpretation → hypothesis → complete recipe → experiment → raw result → evidence claim
```

Only a complete recipe may cross from hypothesis into experiment. Only an independently reproducible result may cross from experiment into evidence.
