## Important limitations

The nulls were deliberately limited and did not reconstruct the complete historical generation or selection process. Several transformations are equivalent or closely related to already documented route, mirror, mask, composition and band-reversal families. The post-mortem records those equivalences and does not count different syntax as novelty.

The negative result must not be converted into “the mechanism is impossible”. It only establishes that the exact recipes tested did not exceed their controls under the declared metrics.
