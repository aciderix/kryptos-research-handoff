Note:
Another simple Playfair rule that is breached in many of the trials above:
"ab" cannot encrypt to anything "A" or "B", it must always be 
encrypted to two other letters than the PT pair.
Exception!:
For example "ABC" => ab -> BC, or if there is a swap rule, ab -> CB.

As a sidenote,the swapped digraph rule also applies to
standard 4-square where there are only two different alphabets involved.
