Observation from Kryptos group:
{
  Take K3+K4 ct. Arrange in rows of length 31.
Original observations:
  - This is a 14x31 (14 rows, 31 cols) matrix.
    The "preferred" matrix for the first decryption step of K3
    is 14x24. This one is exactly 7 chars wider,
    and 14x7 makes 98 chars, fitting the 98 chars of "?"+K4.
  - The known JS notes show this configuration, and
    there are suspicions of folds in that paper around the
    columns 24 and 7, suggesting there is something about it.
  - Near the assumed fold around col 24,
    one can identify a scrambled "ID BY ROWS".
    The position is close to the original appearance in the
    end of K2 (which would be on the end of the previous row before K3).
  - Near the middle one can read backwards: "ETYOIE", which is
    very close to the reconstructed keyword "ELYOIE" that would decrypt
    NYPVTT in K4 to BERLIN under K1+K2 assumptions.
    (Note that this one is read horizontally and therefore
     is visible independently of any realignment of rows.)
  - Not surprising, the ISEE;OHNNYNASH from alignment 30
    can still be read diagonally.
  - Obviously, the famous ENDYAHR fits nicely in the first 7 columns
    in the first row.
}
