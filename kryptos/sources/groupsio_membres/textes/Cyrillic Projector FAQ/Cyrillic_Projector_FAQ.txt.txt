Answers:

1.  Q:  What is the Cyrillic Projector?
    A:  The Cyrillic Projector is an encrypted sculpture that 
        was created in 1990 by Jim Sanborn and installed in 
        its current location in 1997.  

2.  Q:  Who created the Cyrillic Projector?
    A:  The Cyrillic Projector was created by Jim Sanborn.  Other works
        include the Kryptos, Binary Systems, Antipodes, and Circulating Capitol.

3.  Q:  Where is the Cyrillic Projector?
    A:  Charlotte, North Carolina, USA.  The piece is located in
        the plaza between UNCC's Friday and Fretwell buildings.
        UNCC has some online maps:  
            Campus:         http://www.uncc.edu/admissions/tour/tightmap.html
            Fretwell Bldg:  http://www.uncc.edu/admissions/tour/fretwell.html
            Friday Bldg:    http://www.uncc.edu/admissions/tour/friday.html


    3.1  Q:  Can I see it?
         A:  For a photo, look to Elonka's site:  
             http://www.elonka.com/kryptos/CyrillicProjectorAnnouncement.html
             
             Since it is located on a college campus, I would think
             that public access is not an issue.

5.  Q:  Who was involved with the solution process?
    A:  The Cyrillic Projector was first solved by Frank Corr in July 2003
        and independently on 13 Sept 20003 by Mike Bales.  
        (FAQ entry contributed by Johnathan Bales) 

6.  Q:  How is this related to Kryptos?
    A:  The two sculptures were created by Jim Sanborn.
