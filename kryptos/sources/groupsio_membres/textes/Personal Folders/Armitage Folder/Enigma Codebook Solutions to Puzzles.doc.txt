5a.	Quagmire III cipher using Intermezzo and Allegretto as keywords gives 
MLMOJH  FEFHC  EGHJOH   MMLJH   MLMOJH   FEFHC  EGHJOH   RRQOQ  JLMOHM  AFECA 
This was recognised as probably being music due to the very few numbers of letters appearing.  Turning this into Parsons code ( HYPERLINK "http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsons_code" http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsons_code) and then looking for likely matches gave
GF#GAED CBCDA BC#DEAD GGF#ED GF#GAED CBCDA BC#DEAD CCBAB EF#GADG GCBAG
This is the music to Land of Hope and Glory by Edward Elgar, the words to which comprise of 183 letters, exactly the same number as part 5b.
