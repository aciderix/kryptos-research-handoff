 Purpose: Identify all intervals that contain a complete alphabet, all 26 charters at least once. 
 Method: using a frequency counter, in three passes, 1 delete one charter at a time, from front to back and stop before you delete any charter that zeros one of your counters. Then, delete from back to front stopping before a zero count. 
 
 Step 2, same as last in reverse order. Delete one charter at a time from back to front stopping before you zero a charter. Then start at the beginning and delete one charter at a time until you zero a charters. 
 
 Step 3 . Start in both end and moving to the middle, alternate deleting one key at a time from either end, stopping when you zero a character.
