 Preface: 
 The first three lines of K4 are listed first, then the last one . 
 Words that are bolded, italicized, and underlined are words that I eliminated from being possible words through brute force / trial and error. 
 Words that are bolded, italicized, and underlined with an asteris k are words that I eliminated from being possible words except if K4 ends with a solitary Z and except if there is a solitary Z before BERLINCLOCK , which is speculation. Otherwise, these words are essentially eliminated in the same way as (2). 
 Words that are solely underlined are words that fit with ot her words but do not make sense together in my opinion. 
 I focused the most on the last line of K4 because it is shorter , so that is where most of the modifications to my list can be seen. Many words near the end can be eliminated on the ass umption that if K4 ends with a solitary Q like K3, then any word that comes one space short of the end and also uses the same letter of the keyword as Q in the final four spaces is not possible. I used this same method, but with X instead of Q, to eliminate some words that occur before BERLINCLOCK. The word “ an ” can be eliminated if no vowel follows it. 
 I have not included words that would arise from changing the O in the 8 th position to U, then decrypting, or words that would arise if the 8 th position were treated as plaintext U. 
 I have not included any words that begin at the 8 th position on the presumption that the 8 th position has to be modified according to my UNDERGRUUND hypothesis. I may do this and (6) myself at some point and update my list. 
 Possible words are grouped together at their starting position, e.g. all words that K4 is capable of starting with are listed in the first section titled “O words – 1 st position ”. 
 Some possible names, abbreviations, honorifics, and other non-words are included, though my choosing of these is arbitrary . 
 This list may contain errors. Possible errors include: 
 possible words not being listed, 
 words included in the list not being possible, and 
 words that I have “eliminated” actually being plaintext. 
 This list generally does not include potential misspellings.
