I think the algorithm used to create K4 combined the algorithms used in K1 through K3.  I believe the 
message was first encrypted with a substitution cipher.  That encryption was then transposed using a 
algorithm possibly inspired by the  Mengenlehreuhr clock.  The text as it currently stands looks nothing
like a simple aristocrat and yet that is kind of what it is.  For me the first indicator is the clustering of 
letters.  All of the R’s are on the right end.  All of the O’s are in the top line.  There are six sets of 
double letters.  Half of the U’s are within one letter of each other in the bottom line.  These are 
statistically important letters.  The O’s are 5% of the text, the U’s are 6%.
Berlin clock maps to the bottom left corner.  North East begins just below and to the left of OBKR.  
These two clues are integral  to the transposition.  Clock points to how the Berlin Clock works.  
Northeast is Southwest of OBKR.
