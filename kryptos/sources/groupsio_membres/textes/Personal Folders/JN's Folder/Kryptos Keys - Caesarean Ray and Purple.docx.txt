 The process of discovery in researching these concepts leads to a great tie between all three apparent Keys - the Phoenicians (meaning Purple), the Greeks, and the Romans had an intertwined ancient history that lead to the Phoenicians giving them the gift of the alphabet, and all three sharing an incredibly mixed and diverse pantheon that would coalesce under a shared idea of a monotheistic "God of Light" That alphabet started among the Phoenicians, moved to the Greek letters, on to the Roman Latin, finally becoming the English we have today. It gives an entirely different possible shade of meaning to Sanborn's meeting - three men of letters Phoenician, Greek and Roman - with the "tears" being a subtle play on Tyre .
