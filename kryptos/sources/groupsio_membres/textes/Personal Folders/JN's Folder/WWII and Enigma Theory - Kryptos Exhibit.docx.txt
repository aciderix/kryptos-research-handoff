 Kryptos 
 ~ The visual equivalent of a Zygalski sheet which were used to attempt to crack Enigma. Note the correlation of the axis, the tableau lettering, the punctures/ peforations that are designed to let rays of light through. While made on a sheet of metal, Kryptos approximates a piece of paper unfurling from a log of wood, suggesting more to come – and Zygalski sheets were many.

 Geodetic Marker 
 ~ At the location of Berlin wall monument found by using K2 was a geodetic marker that has since disappeared, probably removed since the artist was not aware they aren’t necessarily permanent. Relationship: Geodetic markers are used to measure depth. This relates to Enigma in that the rotors are termed to be "in depth" when encrypting messages with identical near-identical settings, a human error that led in part to the cracking of the Enigma code. This is the final destination of the sheet, the plaintext truth hidden underground, below the ciphertext above. 
 Two things become true at once. It starts " under ground " (the initial state) and ends "underground" There is no misspelling if both are true.

 Conclusion to consider : When looking at how al the parts of the exhibit fit together, one of Sanborn’s old quotes spring to mind about how this was during a period of art where he wanted to make invisible processes visible. Seen as metaphor for an Enigma Machine, the Kryptos Exhibit, show a process from beginning to end, starting with radio transmission by morse , to the translation and sending of the actual text onward to its final unknown destination.
