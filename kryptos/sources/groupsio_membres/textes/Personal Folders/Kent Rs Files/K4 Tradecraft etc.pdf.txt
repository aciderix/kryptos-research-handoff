Text Shapes 
Clandestine communications have often relied on arranging cipher text in shapes and reversing flow patterns to 
keep censors and counterintelligence clueless. Code words and enciphered letters often need to be put into 
normal correspondence, and deliver critical information without anyone noticing they are there. This is a very 
interesting field at the intersection of clandestine operations, cryptology and steganography. When you rely on the 
shaping of text to deliver messages, all kinds of things can happen and various seemingly interesting text strings 
can appear. This is not necessarily a problem if an operative knows exactly where a code is to appear. But 
otherwise it is important to rely on things like "adjacent repeats". So a text shape with multiple symmetric 
adjacent repeats can be an interesting one. Below in K4 we can see two vertical adjacent QZ, three AU or UAs, one 
triple JS (Jim Sanborn), and of course (with the benefit of hindsight from K2 plain text) the anchor points on the 
upper pyramid WW, which makes the appearance of JFK (John F. Kennedy) and JKL (John Kennedy Lancer, which 
was his Secret Service Cryptonym) extra interesting directly adjacent to the triple JS, linked to each other and to 
DUL. In the next "shaped" message we will see the "triple JS" points directly at the W in AWDUL.  The signal to 
develop this double pyramid came from the following K4 segments: 
KZZWATJKLUDI followed later by KZXTJCDI. So the digraphs KZ TJ and DI repeat with inter-digraph distance 
shrinking from 3 to 1. In transposition analysis, this is an indicator that a diamond/pyramid shape is used to run the 
transposition. So that can be a great clue to put in a puzzle like this. And this message provides an interesting hint 
concerning Kryptos. K2: Who knows the exact location? Only WW.

Q-codes QSJ QSS appear in both horizontal and vertical planes 
Here additional players are drawn into the drama via the same mechanisms. The  7 7 (10) 14 doublets invite to 
shape the text in those directions. Note that it is necessary to make the ? part of K4 for these arrangements to 
work, just like it was necessary to make the diamond (or double inverted pyramid) work. This is probably what 
accounts for the ambiguity Ed Scheidt and Jim Sanborn cultivated around the question mark. Is it part of K3? Yes.  
Is it part of K4? Yes and no. Thus the ambiguity. It is part of those K4 clandestine messages that use shaped text 
arrangements. It is not part of the Aryabhata Cipher. 
In the 7x14 array, JS is back but this time as the point in an OSS spearhead pointing to W and ADUL (Allen Welsh 
Dulles). Allen Dulles was in the OSS during World War II. Later he became the head of the CIA. He was fired over 
various CIA Cuba fiascos, particularly the Bay of Pigs, by President John F. Kennedy. Shortly afterwards he became 
a very influential, and the only full time, member of the Warren Commission, perhaps also the intended for the W 
in this array. There is also a double LO, an OSW and a VIG O to ponder (refers to next to Extra L. on the Vigenere). 
And you can again see A DUL and JKL(ancer) linking up in the L. 
Cryptographic tradecraft is also delivered via this array. An important example is spelled vertically in the middle 
colum: CUT (K) VIK. This goes along with the vertical ADD in the left most column. It is an instruction to perform a 
so called VIC cut in the message. To cut it in the middle and ADD the back half to the front. The message is in the 
second half. The CUT VIK is in the middle column and the ADD is in the front. This refers to the intersection 
between the multiple-of-three series and 7squared series. The two sides are also labeled B and A via top row 
signals, perhaps best seen on the cobra and mongoose graph. The VIC cipher was used by the KGB in the 1950s, 
notably by Reino Hayhanen. It was never solved until he explained how it worked.
