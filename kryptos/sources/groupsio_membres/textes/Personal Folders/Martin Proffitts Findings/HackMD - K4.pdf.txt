For 9 years I have chased fantasies and dreams around K4. I’ve tried forcing my own will
onto the cipher, I’ve probably made a fool of myself trying to solve it with techniques that do
not belong to it and wasted countless hours of my life inventing numbers that do not exist,
largely as a result of my own vanity and desire to “Be the one who breaks it using some
magical, yet non-existent method of tranposing one set of letters into another”.
With K4, I think we have all set a trap for ourselves; I certainly fell into a deep one but there
are also two very clever ones, one set for us by the sculpture itself, and the second set by
our expectations around it.
For the second, I feel we are geared to over-engineering solutions based on the premise
“This must be hard, the CIA, NSA, GCHQ and probably every other intelligence agency in
the world can’t break it”
I don’t think this is true. I think its complexity comes from its simplicity and as I stated
recently the bit that makes it hard is that we forget how to think like an artist and infer things
that actually are not there.
For the first of the two traps; we’re all caught up in solving the ciphertext.
Exactly how many of us can truly say we’ve solved the plaintext? I know I haven’t. I might
have found some of it but I’ll bet it tells us a whole lot more. Answers on the back of a
postcard addressed as the crow flies 23.5km south east of

The first of these is PALIMPSEST. No, it’s not spelt PALIMPCEST, it doesn’t have any
religious meaning, it’s not pointing to a specific scroll; I think we need to accept thjat the
word is literally being given in its natural form and we need a very specific part of the
meaning of this word.
Re-Use.
The meaning of the word Palimpsest is
A manuscript or piece of writing material on which later writing has been
superimposed on effaced earlier writing.
something reused or altered but still bearing visible traces of its earlier form.
The part here that we are looking for is the reused or altered. I think both of these words
have meaning here and I will try and demonstrate why I believe this to be the case a little
later.
Why this keyword was chosen for passage 1, I believe is because it indicates that passage
one will point you towards the section of the sculpture that will be both re-used. and altered
in order to decipher passage 4.
Coordinate
The word Abscissa is chosen for passage 2. I believe that this is because Mr Sandborn is
saying ·“You now have one half of the geometric position”. If this is the case, we still have
the ordinate to find.
There is another possible meaning to this word as well; in that Mr Sandborn is saying “I’m
giving you one half of the cipher”. Whilst this is both a contentious, and speculative reading
of the word, it is, I believe still a valid assumption.

Shading
In passage 1, we note the phrase
BETWEEN SUBTLE SHADING AND THE ABSENSE OF LIGHT LIES THE NUANCE
OF ILLUSION
For a while I have been thinking this related to something in the ciphertext but now I am
firmly of the opinion that this relates to the plaintext.
Here, I think Mr Sandborn is pointing us directly to how half of the cipher works.
Consider the phrase, and reduce it down to its relevant components.
BETWEEN something AND something LIES something
Fill in the gaps and you get to
BETWEEN S S AND A L LIES N I
There are two key things to remember about illusions. One, in an illusion anything is
POSSIBLE. and the second is that for an illusion to work, it has to be VISIBLE
But:
IT WAS TOT[AL]LY [IN]VISIBLE HOWS THAT PO[SS]IBLE
Here we find the letters AL, IN and SS but they are in the wrong order.
Place a pivot point between the I and the N and rotate it.
SS NI LA?
No, that’s wrong; think about how the moon works; it always shows the same face.
SS NI AL
This is almost certainly the ABSCISSA of the cipher.

Auvergne].
This work was later built upon by Motonori Mat(s)uyama in 1929 in the paper “On the
magnetization of rocks in Japan.”, and again by the English Mathematician and geophysicist
Harold Jeffreys in his 1948 paper “The Earth’s Magnetic Field and its Secular Variation.”
In the early 1950s, a team led by Lloyd V. Berkner discovered that the earths magnetic field
