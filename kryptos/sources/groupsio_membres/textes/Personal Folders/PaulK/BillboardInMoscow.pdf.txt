Billboard in Moscow
When Scheidt made the comment about recognizing a clue on viewing a billboard in Moscow,
the appearance of J. Edgar Hoover’s name along the right edge and corners of the CT caused
me to think this might be the billboard to examine. In looking at the pattern of the appearance 
of the letters of Hoover’s name in the corners, I, like others, was puzzled by the appearance of 
the V and E, which are letters in Hoover’s last name in the left corners of the bottom CT panel. 
It was observed by members that these letters were part of Hoover’s last name, but a 
connection was not made as to how they might apply to a possible scheme. After looking at 
this for a while I noticed that the E and R of Hoover’s last name might (by plan) show up in one 
or the other of two places as noted in the diagram below. One place was at the beginning of 
K3, at the beginning and end of ENDYAHR. The other place was at the beginning of the row 
where K3 ends and K4 begins, the E being the ﬁrst letter and the R being the last in the row. 
To me, it was pretty obvious that Hoover’s whole name was involved here and I needed to ﬁnd 
a way for the HOO part to become apparent. I noticed that there was a TT following the V on 
the corner. Maybe this would be more than a coincidence, so I did a Caesar shift of 21 and 
discovered that the M after the TT decrypted to H, as well. I had succeeded in getting Hoover’s 
whole name with some small amount of manipulation. To me, it was clear that I needed to ﬁnd 
a way to complete Hoover’s name. The go-ahead nudge was right there in full view.
