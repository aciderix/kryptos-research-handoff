In this, I show how instructions were provided in KRYPTOS for 
ﬁnding LAYERTWO. I believe I will show, beyond any doubt, that 
the character S was purposely “removed” from the ninth position 
from the end of K2 to hide LAYERTWO.  In other words, to make 
solving for LAYERTWO a necessary part of solving KRYPTOS. The 
“removal” of the S from the K2 CT was by no means a mistake.
Ever since the publication of the solutions to the ﬁrst three 
parts of KRYPTOS,the meaning of the last 8 characters of the K2 
PT, IDBYROWS, have only been guessed at. Recently, other work 
being done that concentrates on 12-column pads, particularly the 
work showing a possible correspondence between LAYERTWO, its CT 
equivalent and other parts of the K2 CT, have caused me to take 
a closer look at what might be revealed beyond that. In studying 
the original K2 CT, I decided to check to see if there was a row 
in the pad that begins with the letter S, as directed by the 
curious IDBYROWS.  I saw that the 12-column pad had only one row 
that begins with the letter S!  The complete row, 
S Y Q P F E U N L A V I, decripts to K N O W S T H E E X A C. 
One can read this as saying, “NO S THE X”. 
The ﬁrst two rows of the lower panel of the KRYPTOS CT are:

But also the characters NOS and X are there also. Note, it is no
coincidence that the X is directly below the S.
So now there seem to be two places, one in the PT and one in 
the CT that talk about NO S and X.

K3 deals solely with talking about the removal of the S from the 
ninth position from the end of the K2 CT and with replacing it to
reveal XLAYERTWO. “S WAS REMOVED FROM THE DEBRIS TEXT THAT EXISTED
IN FRONT OF THE LAST SECTION (K4). I INSERTED THE S IN THE PLACE ON THE 
LEFT SIDE, CAUSING THE MISSING X TO APPEAR AND REVEALING HIDDEN
INFORMATION.” Note, Sanborn described LAYERTWO as being some
more text between ...WESTX of K3 and the following section (K4). Hence
it is DEBRIS since it is not part of either K3 or K4.
So IDBYROWS does instruct to identify something by looking at row
S. Row S decripts to indicate that X is missing because there is no
S. K3 directs to insert an S to make X appear and reveal something.
SLOWLY DESPARATLY SLOWLY THERE MAIN S OF PASSAGE DEBRIS
THAT ENCUMBERED THE LOWER PART OF THE DOORWAY WAS REMOVED 
WITH TREMBLING HANDS I MADE A TINY BREACH IN THE UPPER LEFT HAND
CORNER AND THEN WIDENING THE HOLE A LITTLE I INSERTED THE CANDLE
AND PEERED IN THE HOT AIR ESCAPING FROM THE CHAMBER CAUSED THE 
FLAME TO FLICKER BUT PRESENTLY DETAILS OF THE ROOM WITHIN EMERGED
FROM THE MIST X CAN YOU SEE ANYTHING Q
K3 PT
