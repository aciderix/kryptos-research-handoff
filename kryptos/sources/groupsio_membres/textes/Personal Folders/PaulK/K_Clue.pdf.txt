These Ks in column ﬁve are separated by multiples of fourteen. It comes to one's 
mind that that is how KEYWORDS are arranged corresponding to the text they are 
used against. So, let's suppose that these Ks are clues about a keyword. It 
immediately occurs to us that if this is true, then BERLINCLOCK might be part of a 
keyword because Sanborn said that BERLINCLOCK is plaintext that lies in those 
positions in the K4 CT. As it turns out, BERLINCLOCK is part of a fourteen 
character keyword THEBERLINCLOCK. Now, since the Ks are in the positions that 
they are in, the keyword must be shifted ﬁve characters to the right and so it 
begins with CLOCK. So the keyword is used against the 98 character text beginning 
as CLOCKTHERBERLIN:
