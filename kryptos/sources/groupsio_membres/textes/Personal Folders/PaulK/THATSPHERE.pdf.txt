EVEN TODAY WHEN THE SUN STRIKES THAT SPHERE THAT SPHERE THAT TOWERS OVER ALL BERLIN THE LIGHT MAKES THE SIGN OF THE CROSS
If you run the letters together,

Before doing anything else, something must be done to the Reagan quote. The word THE which follows MAKE is changed to A. 
The reason for this is that the expression should be MAKES A SIGN, not MAKES THE SIGN, as in Christian traditions such as 
the Catholic one. Making that change gives
EVENTODAYWHENTHESUNSTRIKESTHATSPHERETHATSPHERETHATTOWERSOVERALLBERLINTHELIGHTMAKESASIGNOFTHECROSS
OBKRUOXOGHULBSOLIFBBWFLRVQQPRNGKSSOTWTQSJQSSEKZZWATJKLUDIAWINFBBERLINMZFPKWGDKZXTJCDIGKUHUAUEKCAR
Quickly, we see that BERLIN lines up with BERLIN. We also see other matches. The ones standing out the most are HE in both
SPHEREs lining up with SSes, the IGs that line up 13th and 12th from the ends and the Os and Ts. The other colored boxes 
show two or more match-ups. Notice in the Reagan quote: ...DAY WHEN THE SUN... seems to correspond with ...SUNSHINY DAY in 
the song lyrics. Also (and, without going into the details as to why), SOV (which is a version of SOL), lies over DIA 
(DAY). Also, THE SUN lies over SOL 14 letters from the beginning. Notice Sanborn’s initials merge at position 41.
