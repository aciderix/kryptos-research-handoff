 To clarify: As most people know, there is a difference between true north and MAGNETIC NORTH. True north is a fixed location found on any compass rose. Magnetic north, otoh, is an shifting direction because while it typically orients in the direction of a location in Canada, its orientation can be disrupted by a lot of factors, most pertinently to the exhibit - magnetic rock/lodestones. 
 A QUINCUNX , while very fancy in name, is simply is and can be a set of five points in the rough shape of a rectangle or square. Think pips on the “Five” side of a die. As noted, a compass rose is a quincunx, as is a die… or Kryptos sculpture…. Or the entire rectangular park exhibit at Langley.
