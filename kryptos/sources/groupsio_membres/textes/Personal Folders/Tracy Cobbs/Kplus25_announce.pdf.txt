K+25 
 
 
 
On November 3, 1990 a sculpture with four encrypted passages of 
text was dedicated at CIA Headquarters in Langley, Virginia.  That 
sculpture, created by American artist Jim Sanborn, is called Kryptos. 
 
 
In the years since then, three of the four passages have been 
decrypted.  The fourth, unsolved passage continues to fascinate and 
frustrate people around the world. 
 
 
To highlight and honor the approaching 25th anniversary of Kryptos’s 
dedication, I plan to release four encrypted passages of my own.  I 
expect that the first three will be cracked relatively quickly.  The fourth, 
encrypted by a method of my own devising, will (I hope) continue to 
puzzle people for some time to come. 
 
 
The provisional title for this project is “K+25”.  The actual intended title 
and subtitle will be discovered as the passages are decrypted.  This 
homage to Kryptos will be released on March 18, 2015.  If you would like to 
receive the PDF of these four ciphertexts, you may contact me at 
ctaycr@gmail.com or look for the announcement and file link in my 
Google+ stream on Wednesday, March 18.  You are also encouraged to 
join the YahooGroups mailing list for discussion of this cryptographic 
challenge by emailing kplus25-subscribe@yahoogroups.com.
