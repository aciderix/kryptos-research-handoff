Introduction:
During the 2013 Kryptos dinner, I was able to ask Jim Sanborn the following:
Me:  The Kryptos sculpture, what does it represent?
Mr. Sanborn:  The petrified tree was a symbol of a forest that was cut down to build the 
CIA headquarters; it was quite controversial at that time. There was also talk about 
how there were tree frogs in the little cracks of the fossilized tree when he picked 
it up and that he could only travel 55MPH from Arizona to Virginia in his crappy 
rental truck.
Layout - The stones and main sculpture form lines that cut through the CIA building, as 
if to expose it. Someone else mentioned that there may be some significant historical 
lines, but I did not register because it did not come from Mr. Sanborns himself.  
Granite slabs – Represent the history/layers of encryption at the CIA much like a book, 
the layers can be peeled back.  
Overview:
	
For the sake of brevity, we will assume you have a working knowledge of the first 
three solved portions of the KRYPTOS sculpture. 
	
The overall goal of this paper is to remove the shrouds of secrecy to one of the 
greatest spy game of a lifetime. Designed by Jim Sanborn and presumably with Dan 
Brown, this enigma has been placed in the courtyard of one of the most secure 
buildings within America. Lead by Elonka Dunin, the group has amassed a great deal of 
clues and talent.

you know, most effective at the same time. You can only build certainty by applying 
learned methods plus something new to other blocks. I find that the clues really pop out 
only after you have solved the key block.

matrix(incomplete), and the three previously solved sections + the Morse code.

details the excavation and exploration of Kin Tuts tomb. So far this appears to be the 
case but do not let this set your boundaries.
