The affine cipher or times cipher needs the times part of the encryption to be 
co-prime with 26 for the decryption to be consistent. 
When the times is not co-prime with 26, there is 1/2 of the possibilities for 
the ct letters, and therefore a different distribution of pattern types among 
the ct letters.

This is modified affine cipher encrypt with alphabet key kryptos and 
c=nested*p=(nested-1)*p+p, where nested varies from 1 to 26,(for usual affine, 
c=a*p+b, a and b are fixed).
When nested=2, c=2*p=1*p+p=p+p so clearly there will be 2 matches for this case.
For this case, notice that
2*LMNABGCDJIEFH=BDFHJKMQSTVXY and
2*TOSQUKVWPYXZR=BDFHJKMQSTVXY
Therefore, there is no solution p for c=2*p when c is any of the letters 
CEGILRNUAOWZP, so only YVTTMFK but not NPZP in NYPVTTMZFPK can be encrypted this 
way.
For example, 
2*I=T, but also 2*Y=T; and 
2*C=M, but also 2*V=M.

In other words, for decryption, only 1/2 of the c characters can be decrypted at 
all, and for each one there is 2 choices for the p characters.
These points should be factored into the probability calculation for 2 match 
odds.
