Part 2.1 - Another One

In part one, an algorithm was given for OTP decryption.  Here we will give the corresponding algorithm for OTP encryption.

Assign to each letter of the Kryptos alphabet a number as shown in Fig. 1-6:

Then, for a letter of the plaintext and a letter of the pad, add the numbers corresponding to the letters.  If the resulting number greater than 25, subtract 26 from it.  Take the resulting number and find its corresponding letter.  That letter is the letter of the ciphertext.

Examples:
plaintext  => BE....
pad        => EL....
--------------------
plaintext  => NY....

Example 1:
step 1:
B => 8
E => 11

step 2:
8 + 11 = 19

19 => N
plaintext => N

Example 2:
step 1:
E => 11
L => 17

step 2:
11 + 17 = 28

step 3:
28 - 26 = 2

2 => Y
plaintext => Y
