Kryptos K4 and the ELYOIE Jigsaw Matrix 
 
By Bernd Adameit, Software Engineer, Munich, Germany – version 1.4.0 
Yet another speculative idea for Kryptos K4 
In the present document, I’m exploring the idea that Kryptos K4 uses a Quagmire III 
cipher with primary key word KRYPTOS and a running secondary key that’s 
assembled, in JIGSAW puzzle fashion, from a unique matrix of 98 characters. The 
latter is derived from Kryptos K3 plaintext via simple steps, including transposition 
and substitution with key (surprise!) KRYPTOS. 
This matrix, which I’d like to call the ELYOIE Jigsaw Matrix, has a perfectly positioned 
match of key ELYOIE with cipher text NYPVTT, yielding the known plaintext of 
BERLIN. From the matrix, we’re also able to recover the key to the known plaintext of 
CLOCK, EAST and NORTHEAST. 
Finally, there are indications of K4 plaintext starting with the word JIGSAW, possibly 
continuing with the words HIDDEN ON CIA YARD and ending with the word SKILLS, as 
emphasized by color and demonstrated in the table below.

Interestingly, the private K1 work sheet that Jim Sanborn released in 2010 [1] already

how to derive the ELYOIE Jigsaw Matrix.

Resolve anomalies 
Let’s resolve the anomalies of K3 plaintext. First, we correct the misspelled 
word DESPARATLY. Then, we discard the extraneous X after the word MIST, and 
finally, we elide the solitary Q at the end. Thus, we arrive at the following text 
of 335 characters:

Starting the Jigsaw Puzzle 
In November of 2010, when Jim Sanborn announced the BERLIN clue [2], 
Sanborn also released his own private work sheet [1] of K1 showing a check 
mark that he himself had made under the artefact KE-ING. I interpret this mark 
as a hint that the key (KEI) to K4 starts with the three characters ING: 
…DINGA… 
…SESTP… 
…JKZLD… 
   
Taking the check-marked key (KEI) of ING and feeding it into a Quagmire III 
cipher with primary key KRYPTOS, the first three characters OBK of K4 decrypt 
to JIG. There are not many reasonable ways in which to continue this trigram, 
the most obvious being the word JIGSAW. 
The ELYOIE Jigsaw Matrix presented herein, which has the same length as K4, 
starts off with the very KEI characters ING that are check-marked in Jim 
Sanborn’s private decryption chart, and the plaintext proposed in the present 
document indeed starts with the word JIGSAW. 
Jim Sanborn’s K1 chart also contains, as various people have noted, a cleverly 
hidden - and similarly check-marked - hint to the key ELYOIE to BERLIN.
