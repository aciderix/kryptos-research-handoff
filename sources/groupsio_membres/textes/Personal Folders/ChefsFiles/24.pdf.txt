24 
 
In my opinion, the first step of K4 is to put it into 24 columns.  
*Also adding some arguments for 7, which I believe will come into play at some point as well. 
 
Reasons in no particular order: 
 
1)​ dYAhR could just mean DAY HR (24 hours in a day, signal to start with 24 columns) 
2)​ The first step to solving K3 was to put the text into rows with 24 columns. 
3)​ Assumed folds on the K3/K4 sheet from the Nova video are after the R in ENDYAHR 
and before the D in DYSHNAI. This would give us 24 columns from the first letter to the 
second fold, and 24 columns from the first fold to the last letter. 7 columns outside. 
4)​ Sanborn’s handwritten sheets of K1/K2, there is a noticeable color change in the shading 
of the last 7 columns.  
5)​ Using 24 could fit Sanborn’s quote “..reveals itself over time”.  
6)​ CLOCK is one of the cribs/hints given to us. 
7)​ Hours and seconds found in K2. 
8)​ NYT articles have graphics of the text in rows of around 24. I think these have been 
subtle hints over the years telling us to concentrate on 24.  
9)​ The most recent graphic of K4, created by Sanborn and posted to Wired, shows the 97 
characters in rows of 24 columns. I think he was easing us into 24 with previous articles, 
but at this point has just said “fine, here it is” and given us K4 in 24 column format.  
10)​NORTHEAST was given at the 26th position, later EAST given at 22nd position. K in 
BERLINCLOCK is 24 positions from the end of K4.  
11)​Q in QLUSION is 7 positions from the end of K1. Misspelled U in underground is 24 
positions from the following “sentence” (including the “X”). ​ 
12)​“24/7” could symbolize the CIAs philosophy to be watching 24/7. (IDK?)
