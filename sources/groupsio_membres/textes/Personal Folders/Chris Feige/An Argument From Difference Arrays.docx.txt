 An underlying array: 
 Part 1 of 3 
 by Chris Feige 
 A pattern can be found while dragging an alphabet across K4; that is, while applying a simple shift cipher. That’s what we’ll be talking about now in Part 1. Part 2 will provide an example method of encryption; a seemingly transposition -like, Vigenere cipher that can be used to make K3. In part 3, I point to a discrepancy with the explanation of K3’s Keyed Columnar transposition. 
 
 First, the pattern in K4 seems to follow the 3 rules given from David Wilson's mores code translation : 
 
 "Inspection of the Morse code alphabet yields a set of letters that is a mirror image of itself, one that is a mirror image of another letter, and another that is not a mirror image of any other letter." (David Wilson, David Wilson's Kryptos Morse Code, http:// www.elonka.com / kryptos /mirrors/ daw / MorseCode.txt ) 
 
 This is how I found the pattern. Here, K4 is divided by identifying the shortest string s with a complete alphabet; all 26 characters at least once . Then, div id ing K4 into to those those parts.  As you can see, five things happen to four characters ,  then something changes.  Five more things happen, then something changes again. The center section has 6 things happen, and then something changes, ... ect .  The three rules talked about in the Morse translation are represented by the color codes, where each sequence is repeated or mirrored or something different is added. You can see they are grouped by columns, five rows at a time, with six in the middle. W ays to describe these characteristics might be; recursion, repetition and symmetry ( both vertically and horizontally ) .

 Part 3: An Undeveloped Party , but still important. 
 
 A study on Difference arrays: these result question the conclusion that the encryption/ decryption method of K3 as is keyed columnar transposition . 
 
 Instead , an underlying perpendicular difference array could have been laid down in the key space before the message s were passed through the password field of a Vigenere cipher. The password s , kryptos , palimpsest and abscissa were created to match the array and act like an interference wave and reveal the clear text as the wave forms collapse . And the same ‘stat-box’ was used to encrypt all 4 sections. 
 
 The first step is understand how to create an array . The second, how to apply it to the Vigenere.
