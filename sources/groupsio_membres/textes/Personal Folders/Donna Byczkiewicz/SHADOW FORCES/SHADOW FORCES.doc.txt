Additional notes of curiosity:
Sanborn used the keyword “shadow” (TEHb in Cyrillic) on the 1997 Cyrillic Projector. I’ve always felt that he’s left clues in his later artwork, what if that was one of them? By virtue of the nature of a Vigenere Tableau itself, the location of the keyword in the modified Vigenere cipher will locate itself in approximately the same place, but Sanborn used a 31-character tableau on KRYPTOS, while using a 32-letter Cyrillic alphabet on the Cyrillic Projector, so what are the chances that the tableaus should look so similar:

I think it looks like a clue.
