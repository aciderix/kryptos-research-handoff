What I think END - YAR means.
I think it's a clue on how to apply a mask for an OTP.  YAR 
backwards is RAY, as in an electromagnetic transverse wave.  The 
END means that column 1 is the end, or that you should apply this 
pattern, 3 across then 4 up, to start the wave at the other end, 
column 48.  Obviously the H gets in the way here - but I think 
that it must be excused.  And since RAY is spelled right to left 
you should apply the OTP and mask right to left.
