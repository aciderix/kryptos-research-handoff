 Last time I posted in Wondrous Patterns, I was at the point of switching over into K4 itself as well as moving on to the next Book of the Aeneid. Tying the clues and separate Puzzles together from the various pieces of K1 through K3, I decided that Sanborn was using the journey of Aeneas through the Underworld and eventually to Italy as a primary source for the clues he’d seeded into the text. 
 T 
 he actual key I wanted was the key to Elysium, known as The Golden Bough. In terms of Aeneid , it’s a literal bough used to get passed Charon on the Styx and eventually open the way to Elyisium at Tisiphone’s tower. In context, Elysium is a place of fallen heroes and warriors, most recognizable, some unknown. There’s some obvious potential parallels to the CIA’s memorials. More interesting however, is applying the BOUGH to K4 as a key idea but not as a keyword for a cipher. 
 That immediately brough t up two paths – K4 characters 1-24 itself , which is a separate topic that will probably follow this, and a second involving the Memorial Garden where the coordinates lead. Playing with anagrams of BOUGH and those other characters brought up BROOK and BULBS or RUBBL, and it occurred to me that it might be the PT textual lead from K3 to seeing into the Garden waterfall in K4. 
 I stumbled across the plaque at the Garden by research, and patterns emerged that led me to compare the anagramming between K1 and the Garden Plaque. Results are illustrated below.
