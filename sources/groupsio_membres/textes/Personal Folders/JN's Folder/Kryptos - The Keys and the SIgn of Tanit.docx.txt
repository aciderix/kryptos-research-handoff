 Sign of Tanit , also known as Tinnit 
 
 Goddesses: Tanit ( Phn ) Juno ( Rmn ) Hera ( Grk ) 
 
 Tanit was the primary Phoenician goddess, female counterpart to Ba’al Hammon . Due to the interplay of cultural and religious trade between Phoenicians, Greeks and Romans, she had her equivalents in Juno and Hera. Hera is of particular note to the path of my previous posts – she’s one of the Titans, one of many great-nieces of Nyx . Tanit could be said to be the Phi( nician ) Nyx niece. It doesn’t seem coincidental that her name is an anagram of Titan - this is the sort of thing an artist might pick up on and incorporate in sowing puzzle seeds. 
 
 But there’s something more important than the Goddess herself in Kryptos , and that’s her Sign, which I’ve noticed long before I got here but never gave a name to.
