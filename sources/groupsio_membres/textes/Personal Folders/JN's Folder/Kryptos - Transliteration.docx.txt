 Phi- Nyx : I’ve highlighted the text. You’ll note the letters NYX encircled by the Russian letter for "EF", and the symbol is the same for the letter "Phi" in the Greek root. Nyx was a personification of Night, spawned from Chaos and Phanes , had two children by her brother Erebus, ( Aether -Bright and Hemera -Day) and grandmother of the Titans. Whether that "Aunt" is a play on the word Gi -Aunt as it is with Phi-Nix, I am unclear at this point.
