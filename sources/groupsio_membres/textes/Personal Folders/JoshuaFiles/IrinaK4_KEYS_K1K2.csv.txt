case, in the Kryptos alphabet. Thus, we have a total of 97 numbers (in two
layers). To start the decryption, ""get"" on the first position in the KEY and
count forward, to the position in the KEY corresponding to our first number (the
number defined by the first letter of k4). The letter of the KEY in that
position is what we take as the first letter in the decrypted string. Next, go
to the second letter in the KEY and go forward as many letters as the number
defined by the second letter in k4 tells us. The resulting letter is the second
decrypted letter. Continue until finished.
