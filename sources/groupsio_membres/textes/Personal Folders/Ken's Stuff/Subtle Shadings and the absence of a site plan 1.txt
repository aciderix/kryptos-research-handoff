"Kryptos Aerial Enhanced" is the Aerial Photo of CIA HQ enhanced with the names
and locations of  KRYPTOS elements.   "North Outcropping" and "South
Outcropping" are schematics of the front entrance outcroppings showing the
presumed positions of the morse code.	

All measurements, details and alignments are approximate and were derived from
Jim Gillogly's photo's (primarily) with some additonal photos from Elonka.
Thanks to Jim and Elonka for sharing.  Some of the morse locations are easily 
determined from the photographs. Others required some assumptions based on
shading, shadows, and foliage. 

I would consider these schematics to be works in progress and have peppered my 
comments with "approximately" and "appears" accordingly.  I hope those who
have visited the site can comment on and validate my assumptions.
 	
Notes and comments: 	

1 The aerial photo indicates that the main axis of the complex is approximately
  15 degrees offset from a true East-West axis.

2 The morse code appears to run parallel to an East-West axis 

3 The orientation of the compass rose appears to be correct (that is N,S,E, and
  W are oriented correctly.)
  
4 The lodestone appears to make the compass needle parallel to the main axis of
  the complex. 

5 The entrance and courtyard strata are roughly parallel.  The axis of the
  court yard strata appears to intersect with the KRYPTOS sculpture.

6 Virtually Invisible and Shadow Forces appear to be on the same axis.  Perhaps
  they should be read "Virtually Shadow" and "Invisible Forces" Is this a
  reference to Sanborn's earlier work??

7 Plotting the coordinates from K2, and using the compass needle as a vector from these coordinates, whe get a line that intersects the axis of the entrance strata near the proposed location of the "triangle block".  Is this what Sanborn wants us to see?
