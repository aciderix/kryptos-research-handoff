Kryptos 3 on an 86 x 4 matrix read right to left. Constructed so 4 68 can emerge as a key 
date in the ensemble perhaps, along with the misspellings in Columns 11 and 22 of K2, the 
length of K1, etc. The encryption starts in column 79 and proceeds to the left in 
continuous skips of 7 columns until the final column 86. The content of each column is 
taken bottom first (79=END) and is then placed sequentially into the art work. 
 
So in the upper left corner we have EN DYAHR OHN which offers some targeted 
interpretation opportunities: DYAHR anagrams to HYDRA, and if the gaps are filled with 
three letters (three letters are pushed down into the gaps) EN NE DYAHR J OHN would fit 
pretty well. DYAHR also works very well with TNREY immediately below. The same HYDRA 
works with the raised letters YA R (Ray) and TN=Tennessee, REY is Spanish for king.
