secondary	  position	  of	  33	  (97	  –	  64	  =	  33).	  
	  
Ignoring	  the	  secondary	  position	  for	  now,	  it	  is	  worth	  noting	  that	  alongside	  this,	  
each	  character	  in	  the	  alphabet	  has	  an	  inverse	  character.	  This	  is	  important	  as	  it	  
leads	  to	  the	  possibility	  of	  being	  able	  to	  calculate	  the	  X,Y	  position	  of	  each	  letter	  in	  
the	  message	  when	  placed	  on	  a	  grid.

Etymology	  
An	  anagram	  of	  the	  misspellings	  and	  their	  respective	  counterparts	  is	  LAQUEO.	  
	  
Laqueo (Spanish): First person singular present indicative form of Laquear 
Laquear (English): (plural laquears) (architecture) A lacunar. 
Lacunar: (medicine) Of or pertaining to a lacuna 
Lacuna: (plural lacunae or lacunas) 
• 
A small opening; a small pit or depression; a small blank space; a gap or vacancy; a 
hiatus. 
• 
An absent part, especially in a book or other piece of writing, often referring to 
an ancient manuscript or similar such. 
• 
(microscopy) A space visible between cells, allowing free passage of light. 
• 
(linguistics) A language gap, which occurs when there is no direct translation in the 
target language for a lexical term found in the source language
