This is a paraphrased quotation from Howard Carter‘s account of the opening of the tomb 
of Tutankhamun on November 26, 1922, as described in his 1923 book The Tomb of 
Tutankhamun. The question with which it ends is that posed by Lord Carnarvon, to which 
Carter (in the book) famously replied “wonderful things”. In the actual November 26, 
1922 field notes, his reply was, “Yes, it is wonderful.”
