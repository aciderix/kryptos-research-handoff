Essential comments:
Since adding the Z to the front of K4, several steps have transpired.
Clues or inferences led to each successive step. I used all three 
keywords that were on my list to try. One of them led to clues that 
resulted in changed text when I tried it and the other two resulted directly 
in changed text.
When I say several steps, I mean that I got through all steps because
the clues I arrived at along the way were the same for both the WIDIA
version and the DIAWI version. The clue to the scytale occurred as a
result of an inference that appeared in the result of step “P” in both
versions. It was not blatant, but I followed up on it. The resulting text,
step “R”, had COIL in the bottom left corner, horizontally, GATE in the 
bottom right corner, vertically, and EOF (end of ﬁle) in the upper left corner, 
horizontally. So the scytale is going to be wound bottom to top, right to 
left or right to left, bottom to top. 
I took one other step (next from step “P”) by doing another 
substitution inferred to by a comment Sanborn made in one of his 
interviews. This particular step (”Q”) demonstrated to be correctly done by 
easy analysis of the result. I used the K1 CT as the keyword. I think this
step may be part of the K5 solution, as it does not seem to relate to the
other goings on.
So, now the question is, how do I apply this scytale thing? The letter
frequencies after step sixteen do not support it being English. For 
instance, there is only one E in the whole text. So, the scytale would
not be used directly with the text after step sixteen. I am taking the
scytale clues seriously as they point to a nearby solution. But, what
to do with the result of step sixteen? 
Because all of the pertinent clues and results are encountered in both
streams WIDIA and DIAWI, including the fact that vertical mirror image
OTPs of  “F” versions are identical, one has to conclude that both streams
are necessary to arrive at a solution.
