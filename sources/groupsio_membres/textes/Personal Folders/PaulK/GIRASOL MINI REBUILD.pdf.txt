I reconstructed the minisculpture texts as accurately
as possible, considering the shadows and hidden 
letters. Assuming that all panels have the same width, 
I concluded that, indeed, GIRASOL exists on both 
ends of the top row of the GIRASOL tableau. This 
mimics the “extra L” row in the Kryptos tableau which 
has GHIJL in it twice, at the beginning and end of the 
row in that instance. Notice what is in the box near the 
middle. GIRASOL lies directly below GHIJKL. This is 
not merely a coincidence, and is why only 15 rows of 
the standard tableau are presented in the way that 
they are.
