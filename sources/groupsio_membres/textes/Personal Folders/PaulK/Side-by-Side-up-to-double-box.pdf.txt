At this point, I want to talk about the necessity of having two streams, WIDIA and
DIAWI. There were a lot of clues to change DIAWI to WIDIA and I was certain that
it must be done. I was just not sure if it would occur in the original K4 position or
if it would occur in the 21 shift position or in other related places in K4 and all 25
shifts of K4. Because of the necessity of having the original K4 CT as a base for 
determining the correct shift needed to move forward (determined in part by applying
the K1 PT to related strings of text appearing alongside DIAWI in the zero shift
version) I chose to apply the transposition of DIAWI to WIDIA only to the DIAWI
appearing in the shift zero version of K4. 
So, I was solid in my conviction that the transformation of DIAWI to WIDIA was
correct and I moved forward to step F where QMEETYQME appeared vertically
in the text. I remembered Ed Scheidt one time saying something about having cake
meaning meet me at seven, or something like that. It reminded me of the words
EAT BEEF along the ﬁrst column of the K3-K4 work sheet Jim Sanborn provided for
public viewing. Since MEETY ME had Qs, I knew it deserved to be looked at. It 
nagged at me for a while and I ultimately decided to start a stream using DIAWI
unchanged in K4. When I got to step F, instead of MEET ME, I got FEED ME. That
struck a bell and I decided from that point on to have two streams.
Now, Sanborn, in his notes, mentioned encountering smoke and mirrors along the
way, I recognized that step F was the step he must have been referring to, as the
bottom rows appeared to be a reﬂection of the top rows. Not a true reﬂection, but
K on top equaled H on the bottom and H on top equaled K on the bottom, etc. At
ﬁrst, I couldn’t ﬁgure out how to use this information, as the system of conversion
of letters on the right side did not correspond with the system on the left side. I left
it and moved along because it appeared to be just smoke and mirrors, as they say.
Later, I went back and investigated more. I decided to do an OTP using F as CT and 
ﬂipped F for key. See my ﬁle, ANKH Project, for details. The result of this was that
