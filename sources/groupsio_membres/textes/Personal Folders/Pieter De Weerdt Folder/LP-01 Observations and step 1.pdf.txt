I N T R O 
Suppose someone just gives you the plaintext of K4. After all the commotion of the reveil 
and maybe the disappointment is gone what would be your ﬁrst question? 
Mine would be: How? 
The decryption or decipherment of K4 isn’t about being the ﬁrst, but about the beauty of 
how it is done. This is also the reason why so far i never cooperated with someone because 
it distracts me from forming my own ideas, observations and sometimes it even prevents 
me from making some progress. 
In the steps i will present to you not all reasons why something is done will be given, but 
there will be enough so that someone can make up their own mind. And that is what -in 
my humble opinion- it is all about.  
Every step will follow on the discovery of the previous steps. I hope there is enough 
explanation for everybody to follow what’s going on. 
If not, let me know and i’ll elaborate further as soon as possible. 
This is not about me, but about you ! 
With all my steps presented to you i’m trying to set your brains into the mode needed to 
solve the ﬁnal 97 characters of KRYPTOS. 
Continue to do your own work, be skeptical, question, build your own theory… 
Fact is that K4 can only be solved the way it is meant to be solved. There are no shortcuts 
and it is more than secure enough to withstand anything else.  
Enjoy !

justice. It is much more than just a single puzzle. Hence the name of the ﬁles: “LP” for 
Last Part. 
There’s also the morse code pieces and the compass rose. 
Mr Sanborn said it is pencil and paper, and in an artistic way…

squint your eyes. (That is after correcting for “X Layer two”).  
Also note that the last part (K4) has equal line ends, and is 31 characters long, with some 
trailing at the beginning of it. (OBKR)

Doing something to the ﬁnal 97 is for step 5. So put them aside for now. There’s quite a 
bit of work to be done before that, and it has taken me several years of getting stuck and 
starting over to discover that. 
The ﬁrst two parts are just text, without anything that can be used as an instruction. 
The third part is a simple instruction. 
Put the ﬁrst three parts into a matrix with the same number of columns as the last part. 
(31 wide) Let the remaining characters trail. Don’t worry that it’s not a perfect matrix. 
Do not error correct DESPARATLY just yet. That’s for later, in step 2. 
As per instructions in K3, remove the remains; that is deleting the question marks. There’s 
no need for them anymore. 
After that create a gap in the upper left hand corner and insert a candle. The candle is the 
word KRYPTOS. 
This is what you should have now. 
The blue L on the left side is just where this extra L on the tableau side lives. Don’t use it, 
just know where it is and how it changes a word.
