 Commenting years afterward on his early Kryptos interviews (such as the one at http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhHFXRQgCPo ) , Sanborn said, “I was very glib in 1990, '94, when these stories first broke about the ' Kryptos ' sculpture. I am far less glib now. Loose lips, you know?"[1].  What is it that he said that is so crucial and revealing? I believe it was the repeated references to “doorway” -- and perhaps also the statement “it unfolds as it’s deciphered.” Here’s my observation/theory about the significance of the DOORWAY .[ 2] We already have a reference to palindromes or reversed strings of characters in the Morse: E INVISIBLE, when written in Morse, has the same sequence of dits and dahs both forward and backward.[3] (Bonus meaning for E INVISIBLE -- I believe it is a reference to the missing (or invisible) E in DESPARATLY.)  The SHADOW in SHADOW FORCES also suggests this kind of manipulation: a shadow is much like a mirror reflection, right? So, take DOORWAY from the plaintext of K3 and reverse it and notice how perfectly you can line it up above the raised YA and R at the beginning of the K3 ciphertext .  Note the following, which I take as confirmations of the correct alignment: 1 )  With regard to the dYAhR string, we’ve already noted how we made the YA_R line up.  Note that the other two characters of the reversed plaintext are WW (which of course is referenced in K2.) 2) The L of LAYERTWO is also aligned with WW on both ends.  (One of the W’s is just a result of the fact that we have the same plaintext string running in both directions, but the fact that the two ciphertext W’s also line up is beyond coincidence.) 3) The misspelled A in DESPaRAT_LY lines up underneath the center of the 3-character string NLS in the forward string, and with the reverse of that, SLN, two lines below.  This is the only place in the string where 3 characters match in both directions.  (There is a 1-character match -- ENM reading vertically -- where REMAINS and ENCUMBERED overlap.  All other pairs of PT+Rev.PT have different ciphertext letters between them.) 4) From the beginning of K3 to the L of LAYERTWO in the Rev.PT we have exactly 97 characters, the same length as K4!!! (I tried continuing the reversed PT upward all the way through the end of K4, and found one potentially interesting character alignment.  I won’t type out that whole matrix here just yet -- you’re welcome to explore it on your own if you feel like it.)
