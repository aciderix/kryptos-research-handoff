OVERVIEW 
 
 
This document will explain how the raised letters Y, A and R near the 
beginning of the K3 ciphertext may be a clue leading to various transposition 
matrices.  One of these matrices involves the CT of both K3 and K4, which then 
leads to several involving only K4.  One of the most interesting of these K4 
transposition matrices is presented below the detail image of the raised Y, A and R.

Why look at transpositions of K4, when we know that NYPVTT=BERLIN with a 1:1 
correspondence (N=B, Y=E, etc.)??  Because it is still possible that the method behind K4 may be 
transposition/substitution/reverse-transposition, or something like the VIC cipher which 
includes one or more transposition steps.

The K3 plaintext can be read out of this matrix by a methodical route. The 
route starts with the SL in the middle of the left column and progresses to the left, 
wrapping around as needed.  In each column the order of extraction is bottom 
bigram, top trigram, middle bigram.  The first pass through the grid extracting K3 
plaintext is presented here in bold caps. Taking the letter of K4 that lies below each 
K3 bigram as the K3 plaintext is extracted, we get this string: 
 
?ZKSUQGOJGKQKLMBPOFUAXLKARKEQHTISTNDQPFTFYSNHIOKBWAEUJUWDSX
RGVFWTINBIGDUJOZCSASKTCKZPWRZBVLBLWOURT 
 
 
This string turns out to be the skip-47 transposition of the K4 CT (with the 
leading ? included).  What clue(s) might have led us to look at a skip-47 
transposition, independent of the pathway through the Candle AY/Antipodes 
matrix?  Recall that the CIA was officially created by the National Security Act of 
1947.  And recall that when Sanborn reused the Kryptos CT on the Antipodes 
sculpture, he cut 47 lines of CT into that copperplate.

final four lines of CT, and Jim’s Sanborn’s released worksheets all have 31 columns!
