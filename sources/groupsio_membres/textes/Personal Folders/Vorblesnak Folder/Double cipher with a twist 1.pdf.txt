Above is the nine line poly table pulled from the middle of 
the tableau on the sculpture. To the left is a copy of the table 
from the preceding page.
Below is four lines of 27 characters.  My final step was to 
wrap the lines instead of running left to right on each line.  
So they read, left to right, right to left, left to right and finally 
right to left.  All columns are read top to bottom but a nasty 
twist would be to wrap the columns, top to bottom, bottom to 
top when setting up the message to send it.
The purpose of the exercise is to build something that looks like K4 and understand the process that 
built it.  I will continue to pull as much meaningless data from it as I can.  Why do letters double?  Is 
that inherent to the poly tableau or is it caused by process or both?  What letters from the tableau are 
used, which are skipped?  Why?
There is a big wedge of a crib in my process that makes it very weak.
No need for sequential alphabets, use a random selection set by process.
