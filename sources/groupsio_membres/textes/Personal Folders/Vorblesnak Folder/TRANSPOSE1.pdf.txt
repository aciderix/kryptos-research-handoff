Playing with a transpose idea.  97 is a prime number as is 31, (the length of the last three rows), but that
could be for aesthetic purposes.  It is a piece of art.  96 however has a number of factors and only 
requires a null to give 97 characters.  I chose an 8 by 12 table.  Now how to build it.  I suspect the 
finished cipher was taken off in columns.  The Berlin Clock clue suggests the table be built from the 
bottom, last cipher letter in the bottom right hand corner.  Now this a transposed table as I have no idea 
which column should be where to show the keyword but this is how I think they were taken off.
Some interesting things to notice here.  Common letters, as an example Q, or B, maybe U, have 8 
characters or multiples of 8 between them in the same column.  This could be coincidence but suggests 
to me that the key has 8 letters.  That means the Q in line 1 and the Q in line 2 are the same letter, same 
alphabet.  The pair of Qs in line 2 are probably a common 2 letter sequence, like ed or st.  Notice the 
first 4 letters, OBKR, start the first column.  But look at row 7.  I see OBK are the first 3 letters and the 
4th letter is one letter further; OBKR  OBKS.  I don't know what that means.
