The Mask
One of the most perfect methods for masking a ciphertext is to use a source of random letters, one 
random letter for each letter in the cipher.  This process is known as a One Time Pad (OTP).  In terms 

[[page 5]]
of frequency and distribution, any text can be analyzed for known consistencies for the language used.  
It's practically impossible to perceive any consistencies when one message has been paired with an 
unknown source.  If the purpose of the work preceeding K4 is to provide the needed intelligence to 
solve K4, then it is my belief that the mask is recovered from somewhere in that data.  It could be a 
specific range of values in order, or an algorithm of some disrupted pattern or something like a grille.
Here are two interesting ideas I'd like to consider: (1) making use of the odd letters resulting from the 
palindrome fragments of K0, and (2) applying the Period Table of the Elements.
