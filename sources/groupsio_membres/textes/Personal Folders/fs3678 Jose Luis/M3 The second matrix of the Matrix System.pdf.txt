M3  The second matrix of the Matrix System 
 
As we already said in the M3 message, we will build the ciphertext matrix, using the same 
criteria as for the original matrix. We will number the columns from 1 to 31, and the rows from 
1 to 14, from N to S for both NK and SK. If in the original matrix in K2 we leave the C of 
MAGNETIC outside, in the matrix of the ciphertext we will leave out the I that results from its 
encoding. We will do the same with what we will leave out of K1, whatever our choice, we will 
leave out the corresponding characters that result from their encoding. The second matrix is

What new things can we see in the ciphertext matrix we have built? In addition to the big clue 
in plain sight that we discussed in the M1 message: OHNNY NASH, I SEE, LINE BLUE, some 
more words appear: TAIL, BEEF, DART, ENDS, MATE, POET, PIER, TREE… just random? And also 
two queens with mistakes: XUEEN and QHEEN. The second QHEEN is formed with the final Q 
of K3. If it's deliberate, some QUEEN is involved in Kryptos. But there are 3 words that seem 
relevant to me: In column 16, row 3 from N to S in SK: STONE. And aligned, one with another, 
in column 14, rows 9 and 10 from W to E in SK the already known ones: SITE and HARD. A total 
of 13 characters... just random? When I had the pleasure of meeting Elonka in December 2005, 
I told her that we could get ABSCISSA from Morse Code K0 (message #3952 and following) and 
40% from PALIMPSEST and K1 by testing PALINDROME as key (message #3967). Now when I 
see STONE, SITE and HARD the first phrase that comes to my mind is: BETWEEN A ROCK AND A 
HARD PLACE. If we try with this phrase as a key in Kryptos we get 70% of PALIMPSEST

(PALIMPS), because BETWEEN is the first word of the plain text of K1. Is this enough for the 
operative in the field to recognize PALIMPSEST? 
 
From her interview with Ed in Feb 2011, Elonka tells us: He talked about how it was a code that 
could be solved without giving away who’d solve it. That clue was not understood. Perhaps it 
would be better to have expressed: without leaving aside who’d solve it. Ed began to talk, 
repeatedly, that the message is intended for an operative in the field under duress (with a gun 
to his head), that is why there are duress ciphers, and that they are difficult to design. He has 
insisted a lot on this, so it must be important. Jim and Ed have already given us at least two 
examples of duress ciphers: with the same ciphertext and different keys we get different 
plaintext: "ID BY ROWS" / "LAYER TWO" in K3 and "T THE KEY e TRANS" / "TWENTY StCONDS” 
in Pre-Kryptos. In the first 8 character example there are no errors, it is perfect. As we'll see 
later, ID BY ROWS is as important clue in K4 as LAYER TWO. In the second example of 13 
characters there is only one error in each message. 
 
The phrase BETWEEN A ROCK AND A HARD PLACE also conveys the idea of being under duress. 
The operative in the field must choose between delivering the message to his captor and 
having his head shot. And also if the TEE in column 15 row 8 from W to E and the E of STONE 
from S to N indicates your position (T IS YOUR POSITION), then you are literally BETWEEN A 
STONE AND A HARD SITE, that is, BETWEEN A ROCK AND A HARD PLACE and under duress, 
such as the operative in the field. I think this is the clue in Kryptos, of what Ed has told us so 
many times: the message is intended for an operative in the field under duress, with a gun to 
his head. In Spanish the expression is: ENTRE LA ESPADA Y LA PARED (BETWEEN THE SWORD 
AND THE WALL) Instead of a gun, it is a sword. What can the operative do to save his life and 
not deliver the message? Well, if it is a duress cipher, he delivers the key 1 and the message 1 
(LAYER ONE). Key 2 and message 2 remain hidden (LAYER TWO). 
 
This matrix with the ciphertext does not seem to add much new information but, as we will 
see later, it is as important as the original, to build the matrix system that Jim talks about, walk 
the trail of doorways that he has promised us and enjoy new moments of discovery linked to 
K4 and K5.
