K2 
Essentially, an apparent conversation between two people, we’ll call them KNOW and NOT.  As their names suggest, 
 
----KNOW is a person in the know, whereas 
-----NOT is a person not in the know (or perhaps in the know, but playing the role of a person not in the know). 
 
The discussion is between these two people. 
The person in the know, however, is also hoping that other colleagues in the know will acquire the messages sent forth. 
 
You’ll notice that the person in the know, KNOW, purposely includes covert codes within her messages, including ED, LY. 
These help to sign her messages as authentic. 
Notice that KNOW’s important messages begin with a word that includes a T.  (Recall the Morse code message: T IS YOUR POSITION) 
 
KNOW: IT WAS TOTALLY INVISIBLE 
 
Includes the clues LY, SIBLE and the syntactic form (IT was TI). 
 
LY--- → covert reference to Langley (CIA Headquarters)?  Actually, LY will follow us throughout our Kryptos travels, popping up in the oddest places. 
SIBLE → Sibylline, or relating to a sibyl. 
 
Sibylline 
Adj. 
1:  oracular; prophetic; resembling or characteristic of a prophet or prophecy; “the high priest's divinatory pronouncement”; 
  “a kind of sibylline book with ready and infallible answers to questions” [syn: divinatory, mantic, sibyllic, vatic, vatical] 
2:  having a secret or hidden meaning; “cabalistic symbols engraved in stone”; “cryptic writings”. 
 
Does this sound at all Kryptos-relevant to you? 
 
 
-NOT: HOWS THAT POSSIBLE? 
 
Person not in the know, unwittingly using, what will be understood by those in the know as, code SIBLE?

This may be the underlying message of Kryptos.  We can recognize the importance of the CIA, and of the good reasons for having such a bastion, but we 
should – they (the people, the humans, that work at the CIA) should, indeed must recognize that, at times in it’s history, the CIA, and the people that work for 
the CIA, have acquired agenda driven goals by using some overtly heavy-handed techniques - a nice euphemism for some God awful behaviour. 
 
 
-NOT: DOES LANGLEY KNOW ABOUT THIS? 
 
Does Langley know about this? 
 
 
KNOW: THEY SHOULD ITS BURIED OUT THERE SOMEWHERE X 
 
Langley should (know that), the (not-so-obvious) riddle, and its buried (covertly meaning hidden, encrypted) solution are on the premises of the CIA 
headquarters.  Here, there, somewhere, in some way. 
 
 
-NOT: WHO KNOWS THE EXACT LOCATION? 
 
Stated by a person who is not in the know, to be understood literally by others not in the know, 
but understood by those in the know as, WHO KNOWS THE EXACT SOLUTION? 
 
 
KNOW: ONLY WW 
 
Only WW, JS xor Ed know (or think they know) the exact solution. 
 
Except for KNOW’s intro message, this is the only message from KNOW that doesn’t begin with a TH.  (The intro begins IT.), though it does include the 
marker LY.  Reason?  This passage may not be that important, after all.
